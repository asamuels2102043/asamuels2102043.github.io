Aaron's Top 3 Personality Traits

  1. Quick Learner

    2. My best quality. This has helped me in many situations where I needed to come to a conclusion or solution in the moment, or where I might have made an error and I need to ensure that it is not repeated. It's importance is grand, there are a great many situations in my life that require me to be quick on my feet. It has helped me scrape through courses, and events in my life.

  2. Adaptable

    3. Not my most refined trait. I don't necessarily handle change well, however I make sure that it is not externally evident, and I ensure that no matter what I complete whatever objectives I have to my best ability, and I have been told that it is a valuable trait of mine. In my life there is a lot of change, and being adaptable was a must or I would have been swallowed up in the constant displacement. It's impact on my life is great despite how minimally I apply it.

  3. Understanding

    4. My most commanded trait. I am a person who likes to help those around me, or if I cannot offer anything else, I lend an ear. I remain open-minded, and completely understanding while still respectfully critical. Gaining new perspectives, or at the very least being open to various perspectives has allowed me to absorb information objectively and offer or come to amiable solutions.

Aaron's Favorite Quote

  • "Just doing as well as you did last time is not good enough." Michael Jackson, Moonwalk (1988)

    • Michael Jackson needs no introduction, he is quite possibly the second most famous person to ever walk the earth behind the Christian messiah. His memoir written in 1988 has many great lines, but I heard this quote very early in my life and it has been a niggling thought in the back of my mind whenever I do something. I've always been a perfectionist, uncomfortable with failure, with being average. Michael calls it the 'try to get what you can' mentality, that mentality of doing only just enough or only as good as what you did before. It's a standard I will only hold myself to, because only I know how well I can handle that sort of self-expectation of exceeding the previous goal, the previous bar. I've really always thought to myself, what if I just do like Michael Jackson? Affirm to myself like he did. "I will be a perfectionist, a researcher, a trainer, a masterer.", "I will be magic."

Aaron's Candid Thoughts on AI assistance.

  • "LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch."

    • I do think that Generative Artificial Intelligence is highly useful, chiefly for summarization or compilation purposes. This has been my take since 2021. The best use for these tools are providing it with what you know, what you have access to (such as encyclopedias), posing a question to it, and receiving clarity on nuanced or otherwise unclear information. I myself have used AI for learning complex mathematics (Discrete mathematics), for breaking down difficult concepts, but never for solving a problem I had. I personally have a large distaste for using AI to compile a completed work, or for handing out a solution to something that requires my own input. I strongly believe that we as people are losing the ability to be candid.
